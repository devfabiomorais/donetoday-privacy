Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DoneToday ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Profile data: optional profile picture uploaded by the user.
  • Workout data: routines, exercises, sets, repetitions, weight, rest times, and workout history.
  • Device data: operating system version and device type, used for technical support purposes only.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality.
  • To authenticate your identity and protect your account.
  • To send password reset emails when requested.
  • To display your workout history and statistics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers required to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database and file storage (profile/exercise images).
  • Railway — API server hosting.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (password reset only).

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us (see Section 6).

5. Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text. All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To submit a request, contact us at:

donetoday.support@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

DoneToday is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at donetoday.support@gmail.com.
